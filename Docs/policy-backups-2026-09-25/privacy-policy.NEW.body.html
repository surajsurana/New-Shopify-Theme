Collecting personal information

When you visit the Site we collect certain information about your device and how you use the Site. When you place an order or contact us, we also collect the information needed to serve you. In this policy, "personal information" means any information about an identifiable individual.

Device and usage information

  • Purpose: to load the Site correctly for you, keep it secure, and understand how it is used so we can improve it.
  • Source: collected automatically when you visit the Site, through cookies, log files, tags and pixels.
  • What we collect: browser type and version, IP address, approximate location, time zone, cookie information, the pages and products you view, search terms, and how you interact with the Site.
  • Shared with: Shopify, which powers our online store, and the analytics and advertising services described below.

Order information

  • Purpose: to fulfil your order and our contract with you: processing payment, making your piece to your measurements, arranging shipping, sending order confirmations and invoices, communicating with you, and screening orders for risk or fraud.
  • Source: collected from you.
  • What we collect: name, billing and shipping address, email address, phone number, body measurements and styling preferences you share with us, and payment details. Card and other payment details are handled by our payment providers and are not stored by us.
  • Shared with: Shopify, our payment providers, and our shipping partner DHL Express.

Customer support and enquiries

  • Purpose: to answer your questions and support you before and after your order, including through WhatsApp, email, phone and appointment booking.
  • Source: collected from you.
  • What we collect: the details and messages you send us, such as your name, contact details, event date and outfit preferences.
  • Shared with: the messaging and email services we use to communicate with you.

Account and wishlist

If you create a customer account, we store your name, email address, saved addresses, order history and wishlist so you can view them later.

Minors

The Site is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing personal information

We share your personal information only with service providers that help us run the store and deliver your order, and only as needed. For example:

  • We use Shopify to power our online store. You can read how Shopify uses your personal information here: https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy.
  • We use DHL Express to deliver orders worldwide. We give them your name, delivery address and phone number so that your parcel can be delivered and tracked.
  • We use payment providers to process your payment securely.
  • We may share personal information to comply with applicable laws, to respond to a lawful request, or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and advertising

We use analytics and advertising tools, including Shopify's own analytics, Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to understand how visitors use the Site and to show our advertising to people who may be interested in our work. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visit and your interaction with our ads on other websites. Where required by your location, this happens only with your consent.

You can opt out of targeted advertising through the settings and portals of these services, for example:

  • Facebook and Instagram: https://www.facebook.com/settings/?tab=ads
  • Google: https://www.google.com/settings/ads/anonymous
  • Google Analytics opt-out: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: https://optout.aboutads.info/

Cookies

A cookie is a small amount of information downloaded to your computer or device when you visit a website. We and our service providers use functional, performance, advertising and social media cookies. They help the Site remember your actions and preferences (such as your bag, login and currency choice), and tell us how people use the Site, for instance whether it is their first visit or they are returning.

Most cookies we use are persistent and expire between 30 minutes and two years after they are set. You can control cookies through your browser settings, usually found in the "Tools" or "Preferences" menu. Blocking cookies may affect how the Site works. To learn how to manage or block cookies, see www.allaboutcookies.org. A full list of the cookies Shopify sets on stores is at https://www.shopify.com/legal/cookies.

Blocking cookies may not completely stop the sharing of information with our advertising partners. To opt out of that, please use the opt-out options in the "Analytics and advertising" section above.

Do Not Track

Because there is no consistent industry understanding of how to respond to "Do Not Track" signals, we do not change our data collection and usage practices when we detect such a signal from your browser.
