# Full-site photo sweep, LIVE karishmaashita.com (2026-10-21)

Photo Director. Read-only: nothing on the site, theme, products or git was changed. All headless Chrome instances were closed.

## Method and coverage
- **Live rendering:** headless Chrome (puppeteer-core with system Chrome), 291 page renders across 1470x771 (Suraj's Safari), 1440x900, 1920x1080, 390x844 and 390x660. The welcome popup was suppressed. Each render also recorded every image's box, natural size and object-fit/position from the real DOM. Chrome only: Suraj still needs to check Safari (see the Safari note at the end).
- **Product pages:** 30 products, covering Mani, Pearl, the Chamakk pieces, 10/10 Signature Top Sellers, the top 12 Bestsellers, 7 First Love, Wedding Festive '22, Anarkali, Barre Oblique and Festive Edit. A second probe measured the main gallery image (`.ka-stage`) against the fold and the sticky bars on all 30 at all 5 viewports.
- **Collections:** all 7 capsules, all 5 By Silhouette pages and /collections (heroes at 5 viewports, plus grid and cross-link cards at 1470 and 390).
- **Other surfaces:** homepage; the Journal index and all 8 published articles; the Client Diaries index and both entries; About and Visit.
- **Whole catalogue, image level:** all 205 public products and all 642 images, pulled from /products.json. I checked aspect ratio and resolution for every image and looked at every card (first) image by eye. Framing was measured with a gridded sample: 69 card images plus 42 random secondary images.
- **Real CSS checked:** the PDP stage is now `--native-ratio` for every product (no cropping, `max-height: 90vh`). Grid cards are 3:4 cover. Cross-link cards are 4:3. Collection heroes are 78vh (1323x601 at 1470) with portrait sources. Article heroes are 84svh, full-bleed, `center 2%`. The diary entry hero is 100svh at `center 22%`, and the diary masthead is 80svh at `center 25%`.

## Product images: how many would benefit from a tight source re-crop

- **Ratios:** 535 of 642 images are 3:4 or within 1% of it. 11 are 2:3, and about 45 are wider (0.78–0.92). With native-ratio PDPs, ratio now matters only on cards: the wide ones lose 5–18% from the sides (no faces cut), and the 2:3 ones lose about 5% top and bottom.
- **Framing (the useful finding): the studio catalogue is already tight vertically.** In the card-image sample, 60 of 69 have the head within ~5% of the top and the hem within ~5% of the bottom. The side space in the column/mermaid silhouettes comes from the fixed 3:4 box and cannot be removed without cutting height (rule 4: no CSS zoom).
- **Images that clearly benefit** (figure under ~80% of frame height, so a re-crop enlarges it ≥1.2x): about 4% of card images and about 7% of secondary images in the sample. **Estimate: about 35–45 of 642 images (≈6–7%)**, concentrated in:
  1. a few Chamakk chandelier frames, Mani above all;
  2. the lifestyle/UGC sets (Festive Edit, mustard, electric-blue jacquard, chintz, carrot, teal);
  3. the celebrity snapshots.
- **Caveat:** for group 2 a re-crop only partly fixes the problem. Those sources are 2100x2850 Canva exports of phone photos, so a 1.5x re-crop leaves about 1340px of width: fine for cards, borderline for PDP zoom.
- **Three before/after examples:** photo-sweep-08-recrop-before-after.png. Pixel boxes on the current files:
  - **Mani, image 1** (MUNJALGANDHI_2024_IMG2265.jpg, 1875x2500): crop box (210, 200, 1739, 2237), giving 1529x2037 at 3:4. Figure ×1.23. Head and train intact.
  - **Deep Purple Cutout Corset, image 4** (2100x2850): crop (756, 1011, 2100, 2835), giving 1344x1824. Figure ×1.56.
  - **Electric Blue Jacquard Corset, image 1** (2100x2850): crop (357, 627, 1953, 2793), giving 1596x2166. Figure ×1.32.
  - Each would be uploaded as a replacement product image, so **Suraj must approve each one**.
