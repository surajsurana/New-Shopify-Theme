1 — Where the wishlist heart lives on the Product page, and why

The product detail page is not built from .ka-p-card — it's the sticky two-column gallery + info panel from sections/ka-product-main.liquid. The card's heart button doesn't translate directly; it needed its own placement decision against this page's real hierarchy.

Decision: the heart sits on the gallery stage itself, top-right corner (46px circle, 20px inset) — not in the info panel, not in the CTA stack.

  • Visual parity with the card. Every card across the Collection grid, search results and the Wishlist page already carries the heart top-right of the product image. Placing it top-right of this page's hero image — the largest, most prominent image on the entire site — is the same gesture at a larger scale. No relearning for a visitor who has already saved a piece from a grid.
  • Protects the info panel's two working zones. The top of the panel (collection tag → title → poem → Google rating) is the page's one purely emotional beat. The CTA stack below is the page's one purely transactional beat, deliberately engineered in Round 2 (Docs/cta-architecture-revision.md) so Add to Bag reads as the unambiguous primary action. Wishlisting is neither of those — it's a third, quieter kind of action (save-for-later) — and belongs on the image, not wedged into either text hierarchy.
  • Kept out of the minibar and mobile action bar. Both are pure buy-path furniture (Add to Bag / Book an Appointment / Concierge). A fourth, non-transactional icon there would crowd the one job those bars exist to do.

Same interaction as the card everywhere else: line-heart → filled rosegold heart on click, no confirmation popup or toast, persistent (not hover-only) so it works on touch. Only the size changed — 46px on the hero stage vs. 34px on a grid thumbnail — to suit the much larger canvas.

Scroll past the product hero below to see the sticky minibar (desktop) appear without a heart. Resize the browser under 900px to see the real mobile layout — stage on top, heart scaled to 40px, mobile action bar docked at the bottom with no heart in it.

3 — Options considered and rejected

Near the title/price, top of the info panel: rejected. That zone (collection tag → title → poem → rating) is the page's only quiet, editorial beat — the one place nothing is asking the visitor to act. An icon button there starts asking before the visitor has even read the piece's name.

Inline with the CTA stack (next to Add to Bag / Book an Appointment): rejected. The CTA stack was rebuilt in Round 2 specifically so Add to Bag reads as one unambiguous primary action. A same-weight icon button beside it — even a small one — reintroduces the "which button is the real one" ambiguity that rebuild was meant to resolve. Wishlisting is also not a purchase-path action; grouping it with buy-path buttons misrepresents what it does.

Floating on the gallery stage (chosen): the only option that adds a save affordance without touching either of the two zones the rest of the page was deliberately built to keep clean, and the only one that mirrors the card's existing top-right placement one-for-one.
