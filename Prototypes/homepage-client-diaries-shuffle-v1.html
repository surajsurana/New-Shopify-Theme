Selection rules applied

1. Eligible = portrait (aspect ≤ 0.85), from consented, published diaries, not curated out.
2. Unseen-first: images this browser has not been shown yet rank first (localStorage); when all seen, reset.
3. Max 2 images from one diary; at least 2 diaries when available; avoid two from the same look.
4. Pinned/lead diary (if set) always takes the large tile.
5. Stable for the session (sessionStorage); new set on next visit.
6. Dissolve: one tile at a time, ~9s apart, only while visible, max 4 times, then rest.
